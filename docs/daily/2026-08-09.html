ChatGPT starts blocking direct requests to copy an author's styleHacker News

OpenAI has updated ChatGPT to refuse requests that ask the model to directly replicate the distinctive voice or style of a named living author, though the model will still attempt to produce writing that captures a "similar feeling." The policy change addresses longstanding concerns from writers' groups that large language models effectively reproduce copyrighted stylistic elements without compensation or consent. The distinction between "copying" and "capturing a feeling" is likely to remain contested, as courts and regulators continue to examine where style imitation crosses into infringement.
