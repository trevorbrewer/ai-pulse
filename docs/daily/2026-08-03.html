Open-Source Tools

AirLLM 70B inference with single 4GB GPUHacker News

AirLLM is an open-source library that enables inference on 70-billion-parameter language models using a single GPU with as little as 4 GB of VRAM, well below the memory typically required for models of that scale. It achieves this by splitting model layers and streaming them through GPU memory sequentially rather than loading the full model at once, accepting a trade-off in inference speed. The repository targets researchers and developers who need to run large models locally without access to high-end multi-GPU hardware.

PISIGuard: Protect your personal and sensitive info when you chat with AIHacker News

PISIGuard is an open-source tool that intercepts prompts before they are sent to AI chat services and redacts or masks personally identifiable and sensitive information. EU rules on AI models become enforceable. What's going to change?Hacker News

The European Union's AI Act provisions governing general-purpose AI models became enforceable on August 2, 2026, marking the first time binding rules apply specifically to foundation model providers operating in the EU. Under the rules, providers of models trained on more than 10^25 FLOPs must submit to additional transparency and safety requirements, including model evaluations and adversarial testing. Companies that fail to comply face fines of up to 3 percent of global annual turnover, and the EU AI Office takes on a central enforcement role.
