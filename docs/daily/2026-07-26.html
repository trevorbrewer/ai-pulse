AI isn't killing consulting. It's killing time as a proxy for valueHacker News

A commentary by Mark Wilson argues that AI's principal disruption to the consulting industry is not eliminating the profession but undermining the billable-hour model, in which time spent historically served as a stand-in for value delivered. The piece positions this as a structural pricing problem rather than an existential threat, suggesting firms will need to shift to outcome-based billing.

AMD publishes machine-readable ISA so frontier models can write its GPU kernelsHacker News

AMD has published a machine-readable version of its GPU instruction set architecture (ISA) as part of a project called ROCm AI, with the explicit goal of enabling large language models to generate optimized GPU kernels for AMD hardware. The move is a direct response to NVIDIA's entrenched CUDA ecosystem, which benefits from years of training data and developer familiarity that AI coding tools have absorbed. By providing structured, machine-readable ISA documentation, AMD aims to make it easier for LLMs to produce correct and performant AMD-specific code without relying on the sparse ROCm examples currently available in public datasets.

Energy & Environment

Wind turbine is being used to produce zero-carbon "green ammonia" fertilizerHacker News

A project described in Energies Media is using a wind turbine to power the production of ammonia fertilizer through electrolysis and the Haber-Bosch process, using only wind, water, and air as inputs. Unlike conventional ammonia production, which relies heavily on natural gas and accounts for roughly 1-2% of global CO2 emissions, this approach produces no direct carbon emissions. The project is notable as a demonstration that wind energy can be converted into a storable, high-value chemical product rather than exported to the grid.
