More than just code reviewSimon Willison

Simon Willison argued that the core skill for working productively with coding agents is the ability to confidently instruct them and then verify their output, which does not always require reading every line of code they produce. He contends that line-by-line eyeballing has never been the most effective verification method, and that developers who can direct and audit agents at a higher level will be more effective than those who default to exhaustive manual review. The piece is framed as a practical reframing of what developer oversight looks like when working alongside automated coding tools.

NanoGPT Speedrun FrontierHacker News

PrimeIntellect published a tracker of the NanoGPT speedrun frontier, a community benchmark focused on training a small GPT-style model to a target validation loss on Shakespeare text in the least wall-clock time possible. The speedrun has become a reference point for evaluating hardware efficiency, optimizer choices, and low-level training tricks, with successive record holders documenting their methods publicly. Because the task is small enough to be reproduced on consumer hardware, it functions as an accessible but meaningful proxy for training efficiency research.

Industry News

Dutch regulator fines Uber €825M for letting AI deactivate driver accounts

The Dutch Data Protection Authority has fined Uber €825 million for allowing an automated algorithm to deactivate driver accounts without adequate human oversight or transparent explanation. The case is notable because it represents one of the largest fines levied against a company specifically for delegating consequential employment decisions to an automated system. Regulators found that affected drivers were not given sufficient information about how the algorithm reached its conclusions, violating EU data protection requirements around automated decision-making. The ruling sets a precedent for how gig-economy platforms may be required to handle AI-driven enforcement actions against workers.
