DFlash 2: Keep Drafting ParallelHacker News

Inco AI published DFlash 2, a technical report describing improvements to speculative decoding for large language models, with a focus on maintaining parallelism during the drafting phase. Speculative decoding accelerates inference by generating candidate tokens with a smaller draft model and then verifying them with the larger target model in parallel. DFlash 2 introduces methods to keep draft generation from becoming a serial bottleneck, which the authors argue improves end-to-end throughput.
