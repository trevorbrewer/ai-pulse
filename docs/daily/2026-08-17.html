Research Papers

Red queen hypothesis – A new way forward for self-improving AIHacker News

Researchers at the University of Cambridge's Computer Laboratory published work applying the evolutionary Red Queen hypothesis to the problem of self-improving AI systems. The Red Queen hypothesis, originally from evolutionary biology, describes how organisms must continuously adapt simply to maintain fitness relative to co-evolving competitors. The Cambridge team proposes that AI systems could use analogous co-evolutionary pressure — pitting models against adaptive adversaries — as a mechanism to drive sustained capability improvement without relying on fixed benchmark targets.

MathCode, Mathematical Coding AgentHacker News
