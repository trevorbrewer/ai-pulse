Learning to Trace Seiberg DualitiesarXiv

Researchers have published a paper on arXiv exploring the use of machine learning to identify Seiberg dualities, a class of equivalences between quantum field theories that are computationally difficult to establish even when the governing rules are fully known. The work addresses a practical bottleneck in theoretical physics: given two distinct-looking physical systems, determining whether they are dual is a non-trivial computational problem. By training models to trace these dualities, the authors aim to automate a process that would otherwise require significant expert effort. The paper is notable for applying learned representations to a problem in formal high-energy physics, extending the reach of data-driven methods into abstract mathematical structures.
