Research and Benchmarks

Needle: The benchmark your search engine can't memorizeHacker News

Keenable AI has published Needle, a benchmark designed to evaluate retrieval and search systems in ways that resist dataset memorization. Standard benchmarks are vulnerable to contamination when models or search engines are trained on queries that overlap with the test set, inflating reported scores. Needle addresses this by generating evaluation queries dynamically, making it harder for systems to achieve high marks through memorization rather than genuine retrieval capability.

Zonemaps(Oracle) vs BRIN Indexes(Postgres)Hacker News

A technical post from DeepSQL examines the architectural differences between Oracle's Zonemap indexing and PostgreSQL's BRIN (Block Range INdex) indexes, arguing that Postgres has a meaningful gap in its metadata-based pruning capabilities. Zonemaps store min/max statistics at a finer granularity and integrate more tightly with the query optimizer than BRIN, which can limit Postgres's ability to skip large ranges of data during scans. The analysis is relevant to practitioners running analytical workloads on Postgres who are weighing it against Oracle or columnar databases with native zonemap support.
