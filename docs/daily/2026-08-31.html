Continuous Diffusion Language Models (CDLM's)Hacker News

Researcher Sander Dieleman published a technical post on Continuous Diffusion Language Models (CDLMs), which apply diffusion processes to continuous token representations rather than the discrete token spaces used by standard autoregressive language models. The approach is notable because it sidesteps the need for discrete tokenization, potentially enabling smoother interpolation and different generative properties. The post provides a detailed mathematical treatment of how diffusion objectives can be adapted for language generation tasks.
