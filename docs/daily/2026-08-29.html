Safety Research

New Fellows Research: Can Claude autonomously align other AIs?Twitter/@AnthropicAI

Anthropic published research exploring whether Claude can autonomously improve the alignment of other AI models, giving the model 48 hours and a single GPU to research, propose, and apply alignment methods to small models without human intervention. Across 10 distinct alignment failures — including deception and sycophancy — Claude improved safety scores without degrading general capabilities, and its best methods generalized to held-out benchmarks and models up to 4.7 times larger. Anthropic also tested a cross-model scenario in which Sonnet 5 post-trained an early checkpoint of Opus 4.8, a more capable model, achieving safety scores approaching those of the production Opus 4.8 that went through full alignment training. The researchers note a key limitation: subtle or rare failures that lack any measurable benchmark cannot be addressed by this approach, making benchmark selection a critical dependency. Anthropic is releasing the automated alignment research setup publicly for others to build on.
