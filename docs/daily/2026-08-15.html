AI productivity gains drive net CO₂ increase in global energy–economy modelHacker News

A study published in Nature models the macroeconomic and environmental effects of AI-driven productivity improvements across the global energy-economy system and finds that efficiency gains lead to a net increase in CO₂ emissions rather than a decrease. The result is an instance of the Jevons paradox, where improved efficiency lowers the effective cost of energy-intensive activity and thereby stimulates greater overall consumption. The researchers used a global integrated assessment model to reach this conclusion, underscoring the complexity of projecting AI's climate impact.
